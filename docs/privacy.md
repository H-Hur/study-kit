# Study Kit privacy notice

Effective date: October 9, 2026. Publisher: Hyeonoh Hur.

Study Kit is a bundle of instructions and templates for Claude Code and Codex.
It does not operate a publisher-hosted application server, user account system,
analytics service, or telemetry endpoint. The package does not automatically send
learner profiles, conversations, sources, textbooks, or study records to its publisher.

## Data used during a study project

At your request, your AI host may read your background, learning goals, supplied
conversation excerpts, source material, and project files. It may create profiles,
plans, notes, reports, and textbooks in the project you choose. Those files and
conversations are handled by your host and storage environment under their own
policies and settings. Using local files does not mean model processing is offline.

Source research, document conversion, optional editing tools, and authorized
external delivery may involve tools or services you enable. Their providers' data
handling policies apply. Study Kit does not bundle these services or automatically
create accounts with them. Review the host's permissions before granting access.

## Retention and control

The publisher does not receive or retain study-project data through a Study Kit
backend. You control project files in your storage environment. Use your host's
controls to manage chat history, uploaded files, and provider-held data. Deleting
local files does not delete copies held by your host or services you chose to use.

## Voluntary feedback

If you submit a GitHub issue or other public repository contribution, its contents
are visible to the publisher and other visitors and are processed under GitHub's
policies. Share minimal, non-sensitive examples; do not post private conversations,
learner profiles, or textbooks. For a privacy question, open a minimal issue at
https://github.com/H-Hur/study-kit/issues without including personal study data.

Updates to this notice are recorded in this public repository. This notice describes
the Study Kit package; it does not replace your AI host's or third-party policies.
