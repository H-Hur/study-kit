# Toolbox log — a record of agent and skill use, and what came of it

Add an entry whenever a new tool is used, and whenever an effect or a trap is established.
This record is the only basis for improving the kit for the next learner.

That improvement does not have to stop at this project. An entry that is about the kit rather
than about your subject — a clause that did not hold, a step you had to fill in yourself, a
trap nothing warned you about, or a way you built to check whether the study had landed —
**helps improve the kit for whoever studies next**, and there is a route for sending it back:
<https://github.com/H-Hur/study-kit/issues/new/choose>. Send the entry, not your study
material — the shape of what happened, with your field left out.
