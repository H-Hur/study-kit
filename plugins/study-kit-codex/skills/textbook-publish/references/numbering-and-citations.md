# Numbering and citation checks before publication

Updated 2026-10-09. Run before every updated edition is marked published, even when
no grammar or English-polishing pass is requested. Also establish a clean baseline
for the first edition. Check the final source and every delivered output variant;
conversion can lose labels or references that were correct in the master.

## Numbering

Check the complete heading hierarchy and its sequence; figure and table numbers and
captions; standalone equation numbers; table column and row labels; footnote numbers;
and all in-text references to those targets. Check uniqueness within each numbering
system, missing or unintended repeated numbers, deleted targets, range membership,
and the absence of unresolved fields or placeholders. Confirm number placement and
format in the rendered output, including right-aligned equation numbers and unnumbered
inline mathematics. Use [safe renumbering](../../textbook-revision/references/renumbering.md)
when changing labels; do not repair numbering with cascading global replacements.
Exercise variants can retain master target numbers with documented gaps from omitted
answers; a retained reference must still resolve or be rewritten for that variant.

## Citations and quotations

Check every in-text citation against the bibliography and every cited bibliography
entry against the verified source index: identifiers, author/year or numeric labels,
ordering required by the chosen citation style, and relevant page/section locators.
Check footnote targets and links. Resolve missing entries, duplicate identifiers,
wrong-source links, and citations left behind after deletions. An intentionally
uncited further-reading list is allowed when clearly distinguished from cited works.

Verify new or changed quotations against the original, including wording, attribution,
location, omissions, and qualifications. For new or rewritten factual sentences,
verify all their claims, including retained ones, under the
[claim verification rules](../../../docs/evidence-verification.md). Reuse an existing
verified source/location for unchanged quotations and claims when the content and
source version are unchanged; missing or conflicting evidence needs rechecking.
A correctly formatted citation alone does not establish support for the claim.

## Record and resolve

Record numbering and citation results separately in the edition README, with checked
formats, findings, fixes, and unresolved issues. Correct problems and repeat affected
checks after later edits or conversion. Do not mark the edition published with unresolved
required numbering/citation checks. Optional grammar editing has a separate schedule
and skipped-edition count; skipping it does not skip these checks.
